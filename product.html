Oud Nocturne Extrait

$195

Matured for eighteen months in charred French oak casks. A contemplative, resinous composition distilled in Grasse, pairing smoldering Cambodian agarwood and frankincense with the velvet warmth of midnight Centifolia rose.

Top Smoked Frankincense, Saffron, Pink Pepper
Heart Grasse Centifolia Rose, Birch Tar, Aged Patchouli
Base Cambodian Oud, Black Amber, Royal Suede
Select Volume Refillable Glass Flacon
Formulation & Provenance expand_more

Compounded in Grasse using organic wheat alcohol and macerated for 12 weeks prior to cold filtration. Free from synthetic dyes and UV stabilizers.

Complimentary Sampling & Delivery expand_more

Every full-size flacon arrives with a matching 2ml discovery vial so you may experience the scent on skin before unsealing the primary presentation box.
